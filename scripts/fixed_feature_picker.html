Goal. Mark a few permanently fixed room features (door frame corners, wall panel corners, ceiling fixtures, pillar edges, wall/floor junctions) that are identical in the real recording and in the 3DGS scene. Do not use gates, mats, the curtain, chairs or anything that moved.

Render clicks define where the feature is in the 3DGS scene. Click the feature in the render of two frames taken from clearly different positions (a second or more apart, so there is parallax); the picker triangulates it and shows the residuals. With one render click only, the point is a depth estimate (ray intersected with the local rendered surface) and the dashed circle can drift in other frames; add the second click.

Real clicks are the observations. Click the same physical point in as many real frames as you can, over all four runs and from different viewpoints. Three or more per run per feature is a good target. Dashed circles show where the current alignment predicts the feature; the search will move the whole scene so predictions meet your real clicks.

Mouse. Left click places or moves the selected feature's marker in that panel (one per feature per frame per panel). With no feature selected, a click creates a new feature (rename it in the side panel). Wheel zooms around the cursor, right-drag pans. The loupe shows an 8× view of the cursor.

Keys. , . previous / next frame, < > ±10, arrows nudge the selected marker by 0.25 px (Shift 1 px), Delete removes it, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Shift+Z redo, 1–9 select a feature, n new feature, Esc deselect marker. The × next to an observation in the side panel removes that click.

Coordinates. Real frames are shown exactly as recorded, and renders are produced in the same raw geometry with the calibrated lens model, so both panels distort the same way and clicks are stored in raw pixel coordinates. Beyond 199 px from the principal point (the bottom corners) the supplied lens model is extrapolated, so avoid placing clicks there. All geometry converts clicks to pinhole coordinates internally.

Saving is automatic after every change, to . Backups are kept in output/fixed_features/annotation_backups.

Proposals are automatically detected candidates (corners matched between render and real image around the current alignment). Accept the ones that are truly the same fixed physical point; reject the rest. Accepted proposals become normal features you can edit.
